**AEBAS means Aadhaar Enabled Biometric Attendance System.** It is an Aadhaar-linked attendance platform used by participating government organisations. An AEBAS attendance ID belongs to that attendance workflow; it is not a PGAK account and should not be confused with an Aadhaar number.

**Looking for login, registration or an attendance-ID problem?** Use your organisation's official attendance portal or contact its nodal officer. PGAK cannot issue, recover or reset an AEBAS ID. Start from the [official attendance portal](https://attendance.gov.in/) and follow your department's instructions.

## What does AEBAS do?

NIC describes AadhaarBAS as a service for enrolled government employees, with attendance marked through supported biometric or face-authentication workflows. Its [official app explainer](https://informatics.nic.in/files/websites/october-2025/aadhaarbas.php) describes the distinction between an attendance ID and the identity-authentication step.

Do not assume every office uses the same app, device, registration procedure or attendance rule. Follow the current instructions for the organisation where you are enrolled.

## What should you do if your attendance ID does not work?

1. Check that you are using the correct department or organisation portal.
2. Confirm enrolment and activation with the organisation's nodal officer.
3. Check the official instructions for the supported device/app and authentication method.
4. Record the time and error message for the authorised helpdesk without sharing Aadhaar details publicly.
5. Follow the department's approved process for a missed mark; do not create duplicate records to work around an error.

Never enter Aadhaar details into a vendor's enquiry form to recover government attendance access. We do not provide that service.

## Can a private company buy “Aadhaar attendance”?

Separate **using AEBAS**, **performing Aadhaar authentication**, and **using an independent attendance product**. They are different things. A biometric reader that stores a company employee code does not thereby authenticate against UIDAI.

UIDAI describes authentication requesting agencies as including approved government, public and private entities. That does not give every employer automatic permission for every use. Ask a supplier claiming Aadhaar integration to identify the authorised entity, applicable purpose and current approval route, and obtain qualified advice for your deployment. See [UIDAI's requesting-agency guidance](https://uidai.gov.in/hi/authentication-requesting-agency) and [current authentication documents](https://uidai.gov.in/en/authentication-documents).

## What can a private business evaluate instead?

Start with the attendance problem rather than an Aadhaar label. Compare cards, fingerprint readers, supervised records and camera-based face matching against your entrances, shift volume and correction process. An independently enrolled face-matching system is not the same as UIDAI face authentication.

| Business need | Evaluation question |
|---|---|
| Fast shift entry | Can the system handle the real queue without missed or duplicate marks? |
| Payroll review | Can authorised staff trace and correct a disputed mark? |
| Contract workers | Can records distinguish contractor, worker, site and shift? |
| Privacy | What data is necessary, who can access it, and when is it deleted? |
| Failure recovery | What alternative process applies when recognition or connectivity fails? |

Use a company employee identifier unless a separate, verified requirement calls for another identifier. Have the responsible HR/privacy adviser review collection, access, retention and applicable rules. An attendance event alone does not establish payable hours.
