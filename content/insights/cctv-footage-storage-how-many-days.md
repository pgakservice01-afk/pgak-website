## The short answer

There is no universal number of days. CCTV footage may be available for a few days, several weeks or longer, depending on the number of cameras, recording quality, recording hours, motion settings and storage available in the recorder or cloud plan.

The useful question is **how many days of usable footage does your site need before older recordings can be overwritten?**

## What determines CCTV footage retention?

### 1. Number of cameras

Every additional camera adds to daily storage requirements. A four-camera home setup and a 32-camera warehouse need very different recording plans.

### 2. Resolution and frame rate

Higher-resolution video can preserve more detail, but it generally needs more storage. Frame rate has a similar trade-off: smoother motion can mean more data.

### 3. Continuous recording versus event recording

24/7 recording provides a continuous timeline, while event-based recording can extend retention when scenes are quiet. The right choice depends on the security requirement. Critical entrances and perimeters may need different policies from low-risk areas.

### 4. Compression settings and scene activity

H.265-capable systems can reduce storage use compared with older compression methods when equipment and settings support it. Busy scenes—vehicle gates, shop counters or production lines—may consume more data than quiet corridors, especially with variable-bitrate recording.

### 5. Storage capacity and retention policy

Many systems overwrite the oldest footage when storage fills up. A documented retention policy makes that behaviour predictable and helps you size the NVR, hard drives or cloud archive correctly.

## A practical way to plan CCTV storage

Before choosing a recorder or hard drive, write down:

- Camera count, resolution and expected bitrate
- Which cameras need continuous recording
- Which cameras can use motion or event recording
- Required retention period for each area
- Any applicable legal, insurance, customer or internal requirement
- Whether incidents must be exported and preserved before routine overwrite

Your installer or security team can estimate daily storage use from realistic bitrates, then calculate capacity with a sensible buffer. Avoid relying on a generic “30-day recording” package quote: two sites with the same camera count may have very different requirements.
