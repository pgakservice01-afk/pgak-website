## The app says offline, but is it really?

You open the app to see the shop, the gate or the house. Instead you see "offline" or "device not connected". The first fear is that the camera is dead or that footage is being lost.

Often, neither is true. "Offline" only means the app cannot reach the camera right now. The fault can be in six places, and you can check them in order without any tools.

## Step 1: Find out where the break is

Ask one question first: **is the camera offline everywhere, or only in the app?**

- Look at the live view on the recorder or its monitor, if you have one.
- Try the app on another phone.
- Try the app on the same Wi-Fi as the camera, then on mobile data.

If the camera shows on the recorder but not in the app, the camera is fine and the break is between your site and your phone. If it shows nowhere, start with power and the network cable or Wi-Fi.

## Step 2: Check power

A camera with no power is offline. Look for the status light. Check the adapter, the plug point and, for a PoE camera, the switch port. A recent power cut can leave a switch or adapter failed even though the lights in the room are on. Our [CCTV power backup checklist](/insights/cctv-power-backup-checklist) lists what should stay on during a cut.

## Step 3: Check the internet at the site

If the camera depends on the internet for remote viewing, no internet means "offline" in the app. Open a web page on a phone connected to the same router. If the internet is down, call the internet provider. Cameras cannot fix that.

Then restart in a safe order: unplug the router and the camera for about thirty seconds, plug in the router first, wait for it to settle, then the camera. Support pages from camera makers commonly list this as the first fix.

## Step 4: Check Wi-Fi details

Wi-Fi cameras remember the network name and password. If someone changed the router, the password, or the internet provider, the camera may still be trying the old details. You may need to run the camera's Wi-Fi setup again.

Also check the signal. A camera at the edge of the range may drop in and out, and show offline at busy times. Moving the router or adding an access point often helps more than changing the camera.

## Step 5: Refresh the app and account

If the camera is online locally, the app may be the problem:

1. Close the app fully and open it again.
2. Sign out and sign in.
3. Update the app from the store.
4. Make sure you use the app your camera or recorder is meant for. Similar-looking apps from the same family can show offline for the wrong device.

## Step 6: Check remote access settings

Remote viewing works in different ways. Some systems use the maker's cloud. Others need settings on the router. Several user reports describe the same pattern: the camera works on home Wi-Fi but shows offline on mobile data. That points to the remote route, not the camera itself.

Check that remote access is switched on in the device, that the firmware is current, and that the router has not changed its address settings. If you are not sure, send the camera model and firmware version to the installer or the maker's support. If you travel often, read [how to view your CCTV on mobile when you are away](/insights/view-cctv-camera-on-mobile-when-away).

## Do not assume footage is lost

An offline camera in the app may still be recording on the recorder. Check the playback on the recorder for the time of the gap. If the camera itself lost power or network, there may be a real gap in footage. Our guide on [how to know when a CCTV camera stops recording](/insights/cctv-camera-offline-how-to-know) explains how to find such gaps.

If cameras go offline often, a record of when it happens helps. Note the time, the weather, power cuts and what else was in use. Patterns show whether the cause is power, Wi-Fi load or the internet line. A regular check is part of any [CCTV maintenance routine](/insights/cctv-maintenance-checklist-for-business).

## When to call for help

Call your installer if the camera is offline on the recorder too, if the power and network are fine, or if the same camera drops again after a restart. Have the model, the location, the time it started and what you already tried. That saves a visit.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why does my CCTV camera show offline on my phone?

The camera may have lost power, Wi-Fi or internet, or the app may not be able to reach it from outside your network. First check whether the camera works on the recorder or on home Wi-Fi. That tells you whether the fault is the camera or the remote connection.

### Why does the camera work on Wi-Fi but show offline on mobile data?

Remote viewing depends on the camera reaching the vendor's server or on settings in your router. A camera that works on local Wi-Fi but not on mobile data usually points to the internet side. Check the router, the internet service and the camera's remote access settings.

### Does offline in the app mean the camera is not recording?

Not always. A camera connected to a recorder may keep recording even when the phone app cannot reach it. Check the recorder directly, and look at the playback for the gap, before you assume footage was lost.
