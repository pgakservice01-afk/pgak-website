## "No monthly fee" can mean different things

Many people searching for AI CCTV add the words "without subscription". They have usually met a camera that looked cheap and then asked for a monthly plan to save video or to turn on alerts.

The wish is fair. But "no subscription" is a sales phrase, not a technical one. Before you buy, you need to know what it covers. This guide explains what can run on your own site, what still costs money, and what to ask.

## Where does the AI run?

There are three common set-ups.

- **In the camera.** The camera itself does some analysis, such as detecting a person. It is simple, but limited by the camera's own processor.
- **On a box at your site.** A recorder or analytics device on your network analyses video from one or more cameras. This can handle more cameras and more functions. The [guide on AI NVR vs analytics box](/insights/ai-nvr-vs-analytics-box-existing-ip-cameras) compares the two.
- **In the cloud.** Video or clips go to the company's servers for analysis. This often comes with a monthly or yearly fee.

The first two can work without a continuing cloud fee for the analysis itself. Whether they do for a particular product is something you must confirm with the seller, because plans differ.

## What may still need internet

Local analysis does not mean a product never uses the internet. Some functions usually do.

- Phone alerts and remote viewing, which normally need a link from your site to your phone.
- Software updates.
- Cloud backup of important clips, if you choose it.

Ask which alerts still work if the internet drops. [Does AI CCTV work without internet?](/insights/does-ai-cctv-work-without-internet) explains what to expect.

## Costs that remain even with no subscription

A product with no monthly fee is not free to own. Count the following.

- **Hardware.** Cameras, recorder or analytics device, hard disks and cabling.
- **Installation.** Mounting, cabling and setting up views.
- **Storage.** Disks wear out and need replacement. How many days you keep depends on disk size and video settings; the [CCTV storage calculator](/calculators/cctv-storage) helps you estimate.
- **Electricity.** An analytics device runs all day. The [electricity calculator](/calculators/electricity-cost) gives an estimate for your setup.
- **Support and repair.** Someone must fix faults.
- **Replacement.** Cameras and devices do not last forever.

Add these up over several years, as in the [five-year cost guide](/insights/cctv-total-cost-of-ownership-5-years), and compare with a plan that charges monthly. Sometimes a subscription includes things you would otherwise pay for separately. Sometimes it does not. Compare the full costs, not only the fee line.

## Questions to ask before you buy

Put these to the seller and get the answers written in the quotation.

1. Where does the AI run: camera, local device or cloud?
2. Where is footage stored, and for how many days at the quality I need?
3. Which functions work with no internet? Which need a paid plan?
4. Is any feature free only for a trial period?
5. How long will updates be provided, and are they free?
6. What happens to my system if the company stops supporting the product?
7. Can I export my footage in a standard format?
8. Who supports it on site, and at what cost after the first year?

A seller who answers clearly and in writing is easier to trust than one who repeats "no monthly fee".

## Check it with a test, not a brochure

A short pilot on your own cameras tells you more than a feature list. Use real views, at the times that matter, and count the useful alerts and the wrong ones. ## Frequently asked questions

### Is there AI CCTV with no subscription?

Some systems run analytics on a device at your site and do not charge a monthly fee for that function, while others charge for cloud storage or extra features. Ask the seller in writing what is included, for how long, and what changes after the first year.

### Does local AI CCTV still cost money after purchase?

Usually yes. Ongoing costs can include support, repairs, software updates, storage replacement, electricity and internet for remote viewing. Ask for a written list of what is covered, what is optional and what happens if you stop paying for support.

### What should I ask before buying a no-subscription AI camera?

Ask where the AI runs, where footage is stored, which alerts work without internet, which features need a paid plan, how long updates continue and what happens if the company stops supporting the product. Get the answers in the quotation.
