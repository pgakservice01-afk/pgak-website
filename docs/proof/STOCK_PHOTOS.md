# Stock photographs

Scene-setting pictures on solution pages. They show the kind of place a page
is about and nothing more: none is a PGAK site, a customer or a result, and
`components/solutions/StockFigure.tsx` captions every one of them as
"Illustrative — not a PGAK site or customer". Proof (photographs of PGAK's own
work) lives in `/public/proof` and is governed by `lib/proof/`; nothing here
may be moved there or captioned as proof.

All are from Pexels under the Pexels License (free for commercial use, no
attribution required; we credit anyway, and do not imply that the people or
places shown endorse PGAK). Downloaded 2026-10-26, cropped to 3:2 and
compressed to WebP at two widths: `/public/stock/{file}-{width}.webp`.

Rules: no legible third-party name or logo in any photo (a replaced PPE photo
showed a contractor's jacket logo), no person shown breaking the rule the page
is about, and the photographer credit is read from the photo's own Pexels page.
