# Attendance — the largest demand on file, and what the results page says (2026-10-05)

## Demand (Google Keyword Planner export, India, `content/PGAK-keyword-master.csv`)

945 attendance-related terms, **≈322,850 searches a month** combined — the biggest cluster
in the export by far. Heads: "biometric attendance" 50,000; "realtime biometric" 50,000
(a brand); ~25 terms at 5,000 including "biometric attendance system", "face recognition
attendance system", "face scanning attendance system", "attendance machine",
"aadhaar based attendance", "aebas attendance"; ~15 at 500; ~150 at 50. PGAK's actual
product — attendance from the entrance camera — has its own named slice: "face recognition
attendance system" 5,000, "face recognition attendance" 500, "camera based attendance
system", "contactless attendance system", "touchless attendance system", "face recognition
attendance software" at 50 each.

## On site, before today

- `/biometric-attendance`: "Biometric Attendance Without the Machine" — honest, distinctive; 40 impressions / 1 click / position 8.0 (90 d).
- `/face-recognition-attendance-system`: live copy in evaluation voice; 39 impressions / 0 clicks / position 15.6.
- `/attendance-system-for-factories`: strong; 22 impressions.
- **Fourteen of the fifteen** high-impression attendance articles — the AEBAS guide (675 impressions), attendance-records-law (437), biometric-consent (185), machine-price (173), proxy (170), contract-labour (125), data-retention (111) and the shift/night/late-mark pieces — had **no link to any product or enquiry page** and ended on a `#dealer` anchor. The same defect the storage article had, on the pages carrying the site's largest impression pools.

## Shipped today

- The fourteen articles end on a topic-matched product link and `/free-audit`: compliance and
  legal readers → `/biometric-attendance`; factory-floor readers → `/attendance-system-for-factories`;
  the photo-spoofing piece → `/face-recognition-attendance-system`. No claim added.
- `/face-recognition-attendance-system` title, description and H1 moved from evaluation voice to
  the plain explainer voice the AI Overview cites — what it does, what it needs, pilot first.
  The capability register's `attendance: unverified` is unchanged and the page still says a pilot
  on the buyer's own cameras comes before anything is quoted.

## Deliberately not chased

Machine and brand terms (eSSL, ZKTeco, Hikvision, Matrix, Biomax, "attendance machine price")
— hardware intent; PGAK does not sell devices. "Biometric attendance" — navigational.
No new pages, no capability promotion, no invented figures.

## Measure

GSC pages `/biometric-attendance`, `/face-recognition-attendance-system`,
`/attendance-system-for-factories`; clicks from the fourteen articles to those pages (GA4
internal navigation); the AI Overview for "face recognition attendance system", read monthly.
