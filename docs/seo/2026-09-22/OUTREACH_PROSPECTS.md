Scope: places where **PGAK Innovations Pvt. Ltd.** (www.pgak.co.in, Ludhiana) could earn a listing, coverage or a demo slot on merit. PGAK is separate from Secured Engineers. Nothing below uses Secured Engineers' credentials, projects or MEP work.

Status: **research only.** No message was sent, no form was filled in and no account was created. Every route was checked on the live page on 2026-09-22 (WebFetch/curl). Where a page did not state a fee or requirement, the entry says *not stated*. It does not guess.

This list builds on the historical `docs/seo/SEO_AUTHORITY_PLAN.md` but does not simply repeat it. From that plan, IFSEC India, Security Today, Logistics Insider and CII were re-verified here. asmag is **excluded** because no contributor or press route could be verified today (see §5).

**Ground rules, carried over from the authority plan:**
- No paid links, directory blasts, or "sponsored" posts dressed up as editorial.
- Paid routes are labelled **PAID**.
- Pitches offer only what PGAK can genuinely show today: a live demo on existing cameras and a vendor-neutral technical explainer.
- No customer names, accuracy figures or deployment counts until an evidence-backed pilot report exists. See CLAIMS_REGISTER.md.

## 5. Priority order (suggested)

1. **Free, evidence-building now:** Security Today (#13) and Logistics Insider (#15) articles. Only a vendor-neutral explainer is needed.
2. **Low-cost local credibility:** ESAI (#7) and CICU (#8). Settle the "own teams vs partner installs" question first, because it decides the ESAI category.
3. **Engineering-gated:** Milestone (#1) and Hikvision/Dahua/Milesight (#2–4). Apply only after a real, tested integration exists, and name only brands actually tested.
4. **Timed:** IFSEC India, 3–5 Dec 2026 (#16). Decide on a booth versus visiting by about October. India Warehousing Show, Jun 2027 (#17).
5. **Defer:** ONVIF (#6, US$4k+/yr), PHDCCI (#10, turnover gate), CII (#9) until there is a concrete event or workshop reason.
