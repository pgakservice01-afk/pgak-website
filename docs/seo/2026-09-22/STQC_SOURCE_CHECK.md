## Suggested corrections

Replacement sentences (exact text):

1. **FAQ "What is the new CCTV rule…" answer and "What actually changed" para 2.** Replace with:
   > "MeitY added security Essential Requirements for CCTV cameras to the Compulsory Registration Order by Gazette notification S.O. 1652(E) of 9 April 2024, and they became mandatory on 9 April 2025 (S.O. 4378(E)). A relaxation that let older stock keep being sold was reported to have been withdrawn by a MeitY Office Memorandum of 16 January 2026, with effect from 1 April 2026. From that date, non-analog CCTV cameras that do not meet the Essential Requirements should not be sold in India."
   Better still: get the 16 Jan 2026 OM PDF from MeitY or BIS, then drop "reported to have been".

2. **Scope sentence (add after "That is the whole change.").**
   > "The requirement applies to network (IP) CCTV cameras; BIS has confirmed it does not apply to analog cameras, and DVRs/NVRs are not covered by this entry."

3. **"verified through STQC testing"** (FAQ answer and "What actually changed" para 1). Replace with:
   > "…verified through testing at a BIS-recognised laboratory as part of the camera's BIS registration. STQC runs a separate certification used mainly for government purchases."

4. **FAQ "How do I check…" answer and the checklist.** Replace items 1–3 with:
   > "1. **The BIS registration (R-number)** under the Compulsory Registration Order.
   > 2. **Check the BIS public list** of CCTV registrations complying with the security Essential Requirements, and confirm the exact model on your quotation appears there.
   > 3. **Match the model numbers exactly.** A registration can cover several models in a series, but only the ones it lists; a similar-looking model that is not listed is not covered. For government tenders, also ask for the STQC test report."
   Also replace "certification is issued per model and a nearby model in the same range is not covered by it" with:
   > "compliance is recorded model by model, so a similar model that is not named on the BIS list is not covered."

5. **Installed cameras** (lead paragraph and FAQ 1). Keep the substance but hedge the wording:
   > "The rule restricts manufacture, import, storage and sale; nothing in the notifications requires already-installed cameras to be removed."

6. **"No hidden default passwords"** (FAQ ER answer). Replace with:
   > "…proper authentication, strong password policies and no hardcoded credentials in the firmware…"
